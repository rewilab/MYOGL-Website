Papua New Guinea is recognised as one of the Asia-Pacific region's most prospective petroleum jurisdictions, with decades of successful hydrocarbon discoveries, established LNG developments, and significant frontier exploration potential. ABOUT MYOGL

Building Long-Term Energy Opportunities in Papua New Guinea

Morobe Yawo Oil & Gas Limited (MYOGL) is an independent Papua New Guinea energy company focused on responsibly advancing offshore petroleum exploration opportunities within one of the Asia-Pacific region's emerging hydrocarbon provinces.

The Company's principal exploration application, APPL 674, concerns a frontier offshore exploration opportunity within the Huon Basin. MYOGL is committed to disciplined technical evaluation, responsible environmental stewardship, and the creation of long-term value through strategic partnerships and responsible resource development.

Working alongside government authorities, technical specialists, investment partners, and local stakeholders, MYOGL seeks to contribute to the responsible evaluation of Papua New Guinea's offshore energy potential while supporting long-term economic growth, skills development, and sustainable national prosperity.
