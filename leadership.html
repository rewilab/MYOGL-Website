OUR LEADERSHIP APPROACH

Guiding MYOGL Through Responsible Long-Term Progress

MYOGL's leadership team is focused on building a credible Papua New Guinea energy company capable of engaging constructively with government authorities, technical specialists, investors, industry participants and other stakeholders.

The Company's leadership priorities include responsible corporate development, disciplined project progression, clear communication and the formation of strategic relationships aligned with the long-term advancement of MYOGL's objectives.

As APPL 674 remains an exploration application, leadership communications are intended to distinguish clearly between current application status and future possibilities. Project advancement remains subject to regulatory processes, technical evaluation, financing and appropriate partnership arrangements.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Supporting clear responsibilities, careful communication and disciplined decision-making across corporate and project activities.

COLLABORATION

Building constructive relationships with qualified partners, stakeholders and organisations capable of contributing relevant expertise.

LONG-TERM VALUE

Maintaining a patient strategic outlook focused on responsible progress and enduring stakeholder relationships.
